Privacy Policy

A privacy-first AI chat app for Android.

Last updated: 6 July 2026  •  Applies to: Papoter (com.papoter.app), version 1.0

Papoter ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android chat client for large language models (LLMs). It is built around a simple principle: your conversations are yours. Papoter can run AI models entirely on your device with nothing sent anywhere, and when you choose to use a remote service, we tell you exactly where your data goes. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect, and the choices you control.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. Papoter does not require you to create an account with us.
  • On-device by default. Local (llama.cpp) models run fully offline — your prompts and replies never leave your phone.
  • Your chats live on your device in a local database. We do not have a server that stores them.
  • Remote modes are your choice. If you connect to your own Ollama server or Ollama Cloud, your messages are sent to that endpoint so it can answer.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Where your conversations go

Papoter offers three ways to run a model. You pick which one to use, and each has different privacy characteristics:

1. On-device (llama.cpp) — fully private

The model runs directly on your phone. Your prompts, attachments and the replies are processed locally and are not transmitted anywhere. This mode works with no internet connection.

2. Your own Ollama server (local network)

You point the app at an Ollama server you run (for example on your home computer). Your messages are sent over your network to that server, which you control. Papoter is not involved in what that server does with the data.

3. Ollama Cloud

If you enter an Ollama Cloud API key, your messages for that chat are sent over HTTPS to ollama.com so a hosted model can generate a response. Your use of Ollama Cloud is also governed by Ollama's own terms and privacy policy.

Device permissions

Papoter requests permissions only to power specific features, and asks at the moment they are needed:

Internet Approximate location Photos / media Notifications
  • Internet — to reach remote models, download on-device models, and run web/image/audio tools.
  • Approximate location — only for tools that need it (e.g. local weather). You can decline and still use the app.
  • Photos / media — so you can attach an image for analysis or save a generated image.
  • Notifications — to show progress for background model downloads.

Advertising

Papoter includes an optional "Support the developer" prompt that may show a Google AdMob interstitial ad. When an ad is shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may access your device's advertising identifier and related information to serve and measure the ad, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Ads are not the core of the app and appear only occasionally.

Children & safe use

Papoter includes a kid-safe content guardrail that is on by default and screens every prompt before it reaches a model. The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. Because responses are generated by AI, they can be inaccurate or unsuitable; adult supervision is recommended for younger users, and AI output should never be treated as professional (legal, medical, financial or safety) advice.

Data storage & deletion

  • Your conversations, settings, name and keys are stored locally on your device.
  • You can delete any conversation from within the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local data, including your history and stored keys.
  • We do not operate a cloud backup of your conversations. Data sent to a remote model is retained (if at all) under that provider's policy, not ours.

Your choices & rights

  • Stay fully offline by using only on-device models.
  • Control every remote call — remote features run only when you choose a remote provider or invoke a tool.
  • Delete your data in-app or by uninstalling.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Since we do not maintain a central store of your personal data, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device.
