Everything in one chat

A whole toolkit, quietly built in

Papoter isn't just a text box. Ask it to make a picture, hum a tune, read a document, or check the weather — it figures out the right tool and does it, right in the conversation.

🔒

Fully on-device

Run models locally with llama.cpp. Works offline — your prompts and replies never leave your phone.

☁️

Ollama & Ollama Cloud

Point at your own Ollama server on your LAN, or sign in to Ollama Cloud with an API key and pick a hosted model.

🎨

Image generation

Ask for a picture and get one in the chat, with a one-tap save. On-device Stable Diffusion first, keyless fallback.

🎵

Music & audio

Generate short music or sound clips that play right inside the conversation, with download.

👁️

See & read

Attach a photo for vision analysis, or drop in a PDF and Papoter reads it as clean, model-ready text.

🛠️

Built-in tools

Web search, current time, location, weather and trending news — called automatically when you need them.

🛡️

Kid-safe guardrail

A Llama Guard-style filter screens every prompt before it reaches any model. On by default.

⚡

Streaming + stop

Watch replies arrive token by token, and tap stop any time to keep what's already there.

🕘

History & export

Conversations are saved locally, auto-titled, and exportable as a clean text transcript.

Privacy by design

Your conversations are yours.

Papoter has no account and no analytics SDK of its own. Local models run entirely offline, and your chat history lives in a database on your device — not on our servers.

  • On-device mode sends nothing, anywhere.
  • Remote calls happen only when you choose them.
  • Delete any chat in-app; uninstall clears it all.
