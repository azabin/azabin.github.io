# Privacy Policy for Catricket

**Last Updated:** May 24, 2024

**A for Arts and Crafts** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Catricket mobile application (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Game.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Catricket is designed to be a fun, offline experience. 
- **Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.
- **Game Data:** The Game stores high scores and game settings (such as volume levels) locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The Game does not currently use any third-party services (such as analytics, advertising networks, or social media integration) that collect data from your device.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Our Game is child-friendly and does not collect any personal information from children or adults. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## 4. Security
Since we do not collect any information over the internet, your game data is as secure as your physical device.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
