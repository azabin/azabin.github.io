Privacy Policy

Catricket — A Purr-fect Cricket Game 🐾

Last updated: July 5, 2026

A for Arts and Crafts ("we", "our", or "us") develops and operates the Catricket mobile application (the "Game") for Android. We care about your privacy — especially because Catricket is designed for children and families. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information the Game does and does not handle.

The short version 🐱

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email or location.
  • The current testing release shows no ads and makes no network connections.
  • A future update will add Google AdMob ads, which use your device's resettable advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • Your settings and high scores are saved on your device.
  • We are upfront about the permissions the app requests and why.

1. Information We Collect

Catricket does not collect, request, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any account sign-in.

The Game saves a small amount of gameplay data locally on your device only, using Android's private SharedPreferences storage:

  • Settings: your music and sound-effects volume, and your preferred match length and difficulty.
  • High scores: match statistics such as runs scored, balls faced, wickets lost, target, overs, difficulty, win/loss result, and the timestamp of the match.

High scores are labelled with a fixed placeholder name ("Player"); the Game does not ask you to enter a real name and stores no personal identifier. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising identifier (planned): The current testing release does not use it. A future update will introduce Google AdMob (see section 4); at that point Google's Mobile Ads SDK may access your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. The advertising ID is a resettable, non-permanent identifier managed by your device — we do not link it to your identity. We are disclosing this in advance so you know what to expect.

2. Permissions

Catricket requests only the permissions needed for its planned Google AdMob integration. It requests no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or storage.

  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — allows access to your device's advertising ID for the planned ads. On Android 13+ this permission is required or the advertising ID is zeroed out.
  • android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — reserved for the Mobile Ads SDK to request and display ads once that feature ships. The current testing build makes no network connections.

3. Device Backup

Android's built-in Auto Backup feature may include the Game's local settings and high scores in your device's regular backup to your own Google Drive account. This backup is created and controlled by Android and Google under your device settings — not by us. We never receive, access, or store this backup. You can disable app backups at any time in your device's Settings (typically System > Backup), and any such backup is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The current testing release contains no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no social-media integrations.

Planned Google AdMob ads: A future update will introduce ads served through Google AdMob, Google's mobile advertising service. When that feature is live, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID together with limited technical information (such as device type, general location inferred from IP, and ad interactions) in order to serve and measure ads. This processing is carried out by Google as described in the How Google uses information and AdMob policies.

Your choices: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads. We will update this policy and our Google Play Data Safety declaration before ads are released.

5. Children's Privacy

Catricket is family-friendly and suitable for children. The current release collects no personal information from anyone. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy. When advertising is introduced, any ads will be configured to serve only age-appropriate, non-personalised content in line with the Families policy. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all Game data lives only on your device, you are always in full control:

  • Clear the in-game high-score list from the Game itself, or clear the app's data via your device's Settings > Apps > Catricket > Storage > Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the Game removes all of its local data from your device.
  • To remove any copy held in a device backup, manage or delete your backup in your device and Google account settings.
  • Once ads are introduced, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

7. Data Security

The current release stores its data only in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot access, and transmits nothing over the internet. When ads are added, ad requests are handled securely by Google's Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections.

8. Google Play Data Safety

For the current testing release, our Google Play Data Safety declaration states that Catricket collects no data. Because we have declared upcoming use of the advertising ID, we will update the Data Safety form to reflect that an advertising ID is collected and shared for advertising purposes before the AdMob update is released to users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes may also be noted in the app's release notes.
