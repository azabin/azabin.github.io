Privacy Policy

Catricket — A Purr-fect Cricket Game 🐾

Last updated: July 8, 2026

A for Arts and Crafts ("we", "our", or "us") develops and operates the Catricket mobile application (the "Game") for Android. Catricket is a casual cricket game intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information the Game does and does not handle.

The short version 🐱

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email or location.
  • On first launch, a neutral age screen asks only for your year of birth so ads can be shown appropriately.
  • The Game shows optional, opt-in Google AdMob rewarded ads that you choose to watch for in-game credits.
  • Players who identify as under 13 only ever see non-personalised, G-rated ads with no advertising ID.
  • Your settings, high scores, game credits and age answer are saved on your device only.

1. Information We Collect

Catricket does not collect, request, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any account sign-in.

The Game saves a small amount of gameplay data locally on your device only, using Android's private SharedPreferences storage:

  • Settings: your music and sound-effects volume, and your preferred match length and difficulty.
  • High scores: match statistics such as runs scored, balls faced, wickets lost, target, overs, difficulty, win/loss result, and the timestamp of the match.
  • Game credits: the number of in-game credits you currently hold.
  • Age answer: the year of birth you enter on the neutral age screen — a single number used only to decide whether ads must be requested with child-directed settings. It is never linked to your identity and never leaves your device.

High scores are labelled with a fixed placeholder name ("Player"); the Game does not ask you to enter a real name and stores no personal identifier. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising identifier: For players 13 and over, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may access your device's advertising ID — a resettable, non-permanent identifier managed by your device — to serve and measure ads. We do not link it to your identity. For players who identify as under 13, ad requests are tagged child-directed and no advertising ID is used.

2. Permissions

Catricket requests only the permissions needed for its Google AdMob rewarded-ad feature. It requests no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or storage.

  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — allows access to your device's advertising ID for ads shown to players 13 and over. On Android 13+ this permission is required or the advertising ID is zeroed out. It is not used for players who identify as under 13.
  • android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required by the Mobile Ads SDK to request and display ads. Apart from ad requests, the Game plays fully offline.

3. Device Backup

Android's built-in Auto Backup feature may include the Game's local settings, high scores, game credits and age answer in your device's regular backup to your own Google Drive account. This backup is created and controlled by Android and Google under your device settings — not by us. We never receive, access, or store this backup. You can disable app backups at any time in your device's Settings (typically System > Backup), and any such backup is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Catricket contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and no social-media integrations. The only third-party service is Google AdMob.

Google AdMob rewarded ads: The Game offers optional rewarded ads — you may choose to watch a short video ad to top up your in-game credits (for example, from the Settings screen or after losing a match). Ads are always opt-in; nothing plays unless you tap to watch it. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process your device's advertising ID together with limited technical information (such as device type, general location inferred from IP, and ad interactions) in order to serve and measure ads. This processing is carried out by Google as described in the How Google uses information and AdMob policies.

Age-appropriate settings: Based on the year of birth entered on the neutral age screen, the Game configures every ad request. Players 13 and over see ads capped at a "Teen" (T) maximum content rating. Players under 13 have ads tagged for child-directed treatment and "under age of consent" — non-personalised, capped at a "G" content rating, and served without the advertising ID, in line with the Google Play Families policy.

Your choices: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

5. Families Policy Compliance

Catricket participates in a manner consistent with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • The Game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
  • A neutral age screen (no wording or default that nudges toward a particular age) determines whether a player should receive child-directed ad treatment.
  • For players identified as under 13, ads are non-personalised, G-rated and served without the advertising ID; there are no social logins, and no personal data is collected or transmitted.
  • The Game contains no in-app purchases and no user-to-user communication.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please contact us using the details below.

6. Children's Data Retention

Because Catricket collects no personal data from any user, there is no children's personal data held on our servers — we operate no servers that receive Game data. The only information associated with a player under 13 is the on-device data listed in section 1 (settings, high scores, game credits, and the year-of-birth answer), all of which is stored solely in the app's private storage on the device and is retained only until it is cleared. A parent, guardian or player can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game (see section 7). We do not retain, transmit, or share any children's data, and no advertising identifier is used for players under 13.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all Game data lives only on your device, you are always in full control:

  • Clear the in-game high-score list from the Game itself, or clear the app's data via your device's Settings > Apps > Catricket > Storage > Clear data (this also resets the age screen, which will be shown again next launch).
  • Uninstalling the Game removes all of its local data from your device.
  • To remove any copy held in a device backup, manage or delete your backup in your device and Google account settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

8. Data Security

The Game stores its data only in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot access. Apart from ad requests — which are handled securely by Google's Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections — the Game transmits nothing over the internet.

9. Google Play Data Safety

Our Google Play Data Safety declaration reflects that Catricket, through the Google AdMob SDK, may collect an advertising ID for advertising purposes for players 13 and over, and collects no other data. No data is collected for players who identify as under 13. We do not sell data, and we do not use it for tracking beyond serving and measuring the ads described above.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes may also be noted in the app's release notes.
