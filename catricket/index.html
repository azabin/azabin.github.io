The most adorable cricket on Android

Step onto the pitch as a fluffy cat batsman in a sunny, circular stadium packed with cheering feline fans. Simple two-button controls make it easy for kids, while timing and power give every match a delightful challenge.

How to Play 🐾

Two buttons. Endless fun.

1

Pick Your Match

Choose 5, 10, 20 or 50 overs and a difficulty from Baby Step to Hardest.

2

Tap & Hold

Hold a HIT button to build power as the bowler cat sends one down.

3

Release to Swing

Let go at the right moment — Onside or Leg Side — to place your shot.

4

Chase the Target

Rack up runs, avoid getting out, and win before the overs run out!
